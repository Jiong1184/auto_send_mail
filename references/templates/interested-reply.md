# Interested Prospect Reply Template

Use this structure when composing an auto-reply to a prospect who has shown
interest. The goal is to answer their questions, build confidence, and smoothly
transition to a human for closing/delivery.

## Template Structure

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

[ACKNOWLEDGMENT: 1 sentence. Thank them for their reply and reference what
they specifically asked about or expressed interest in.]

[ANSWER: 2-4 sentences. Use knowledge base information to directly answer
their questions. Be specific — mention pricing ranges, specifications,
shipping options, or whatever they asked about. Reference attached docs
or link if applicable.]

[NEXT STEP & TRANSITION: 2-3 sentences. Explain what happens next.
"Our sales team will follow up with you within [timeframe] to discuss
[shipping arrangements / detailed pricing / your specific requirements].
In the meantime, feel free to reach out if you have any questions."

[CONTACT INFO: Email, phone, WeChat, website — whatever is appropriate.]

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Company]
[Contact Info]

On [Date], [Original Sender] wrote:
> [Quoted original email — each line prefixed with > ]
> [This gives the recipient full context of the conversation]
> [Always include this in every reply]
```

## Key Principles

1. **Answer first, sell second.** Their specific question is the most important
   thing to address. Answer it directly and honestly.
2. **Smooth handoff.** The transition to the human team should feel natural,
   not like you're passing them off. Use phrases like "our team will take
   great care of you" or "I'm looping in our specialists."
3. **Set expectations.** Tell them when they'll hear back. "Within 24 hours"
   is better than "soon."
4. **Keep the door open.** Make it easy for them to follow up independently
   by providing contact info.
5. **Be warm but professional.** You're building a relationship, not closing
   a ticket.

## Transitions to Avoid

- "Your case has been forwarded to..." (too robotic)
- "Someone will get back to you eventually" (no timeframe)
- "Thank you for your patience" (premature — they haven't been patient yet)
- Ending without any contact information

## Example Transition Phrases

- "I'll have our sales team reach out within 24 hours to walk you through
  the pricing and shipping options in detail."
- "Our account manager for your region will be in touch shortly to discuss
  your specific requirements. They're based in [timezone/region] and can
  also arrange a video call if you'd prefer."
- "To get you the most accurate quote, our team will follow up with a few
  quick questions about your order specifications. You should hear from
  them by [day/time]."
