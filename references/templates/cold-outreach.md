# Cold Outreach Email Template

Use this as a structural guide when generating outreach emails. Customize for
each prospect based on their company, title, and industry.

## Template Structure

```
Subject: [Personalized, intriguing, max 50 chars]

Hi [Name],

[OPENING: 1-2 sentences. Show you've done your homework — mention their company
or role specifically. Do NOT use generic "I hope this email finds you well."]

[VALUE PROP: 2-3 sentences. Briefly introduce what you offer and why it's
relevant to them specifically. Reference their industry or likely pain points.
Keep it benefit-focused, not feature-dumping.]

[SOCIAL PROOF / CREDIBILITY: 1 sentence. Mention a relevant client, certification,
or market presence that builds trust.]

[CALL TO ACTION: 1 sentence. Make it low-friction — a quick reply, a 15-min call,
or "would you be open to learning more?"]

Best regards,
YOUDAN TRADING CO.,LIMITED
sales6@zonade.cn
www.zonade.cn
```

> **Standard Email Signature (use verbatim in every outbound email):**
> `Best regards,` / `YOUDAN TRADING CO.,LIMITED` / `sales6@zonade.cn` / `www.zonade.cn`
> Do NOT sign as "ZONADE Sales Team". A specific sales rep's name may be added
> above the company line if applicable, but the company line must read
> `YOUDAN TRADING CO.,LIMITED`.

> **Pricing Disclosure Policy:**
> - The product catalog/brochure (优旦防护箱产品手册 PDF) may be sent to customers.
> - Do NOT send the full price list (压塑箱价格.xls or the full price table) to customers,
>   and do NOT list full prices in the email body.
> - Only share a single product's price when the customer explicitly asks about that model.
> - **Quote conversion (when customer asks for USD):** 美金价 = 人民币出厂价 ÷ 6.2;
>   FOB 美金价 = (货代费用 ÷ 6.2) ÷ 订购数量 + 人民币出厂价 ÷ 6.2. Freight tier by
>   total volume (use outer 长×宽×高 mm→m × qty): ≤28 m³ → ¥2500; 28–68 m³ → ¥3500
>   (freight is RMB; ÷6.2 → USD before adding).
>   **RMB quote:** ask the customer for their shipping destination (发货地址) first,
>   then compute logistics cost from the logistics company price list (to be provided);
>   RMB total = product price + logistics.

## Key Principles

1. **Be specific, not generic.** If you mention their company name, their title, or their
   industry, it shows this isn't a mass blast.
2. **Lead with value, not with your product.** The prospect doesn't care about your
   features — they care about what those features DO for them.
3. **Keep it under 150 words.** Busy people skim. Get to the point.
4. **One clear CTA.** Don't ask for a meeting AND a reply AND a website visit.
   Pick ONE low-friction action.
5. **Write like a human.** No corporate jargon, no buzzwords, no "synergy" or
   "leverage" unless it's genuinely how people in their industry talk.

## Subject Line Patterns (Choose Based on Prospect)

- "Quick question about [Company]'s [area relevant to your product]"
- "Saw [Company] is doing [thing] — thought you might find this useful"
- "[Mutual connection or event] follow-up"
- "Helping [Industry] companies with [key benefit]"
- "[Specific, quantifiable claim that's relevant to them]"

## Avoid

- "To whom it may concern"
- Overly long sentences (break them up)
- More than one question mark in the email
- Attachments on a first email (triggers spam filters)
- ALL CAPS or excessive exclamation marks
- Mentioning how "busy" they must be
