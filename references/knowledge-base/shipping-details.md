## Product-Specific Shipping Notes

Protective cases are hollow and stackable — multiple units can be nested to reduce shipping volume. Contact us for optimized shipping configurations for bulk orders.

### Per-Unit Shipping Weight Reference

Shipping weight ≈ product weight + packaging (200–500g depending on case size). See [pricing.md](pricing.md) for product weights of each model.

## Order Processing

1. Order confirmed → Proforma Invoice issued
2. Payment received → Production begins
3. Production complete → Quality inspection (2-3 business days)
4. Inspection passed → Packing and shipping
5. Tracking number provided within 24 hours of dispatch

## Customs & Documentation
